Services

At S L Gastro & Liver Clinic, endoscopy, sigmoidoscopy, proctoscopy, colonoscopy procedures are done in a safe, smooth and efficient way after proper high disinfection to avoid cross infection.

All the procedures are done using latest advanced endoscopy equipment which can detect lesions missed by conventional endoscope equipment.

Technology like Blue laser imaging (BLI) and Linked color imaging (LCI) integrated with Multi LED visualisation helps in viewing and acquiring high resolution stomach and colon images.

UPPER G.I. ENDOSCOPY

Upper G.I. endoscopy procedure for gastric and acid reflux

HYDROGEN BREATH TEST

Hydrogen breath test for bloating, diarrhoea, abdominal gas, IBS and lactose intolerance.

COLONOSCOPY

Colonoscopy or sigmoidoscopy for constipation, blood in stools, diarrhoea, piles, fissure & fistula

FIBROSCAN

Liver stiffness measurement to assess liver health in alcohol & non alcohol taking patients

UREA BREATH TEST

Urea Breath test to rule Helicobacter pylori infection

BODY COMPOSITION ANALYSIS

Body composition analysis to assess body fat, muscle mass and overall health
